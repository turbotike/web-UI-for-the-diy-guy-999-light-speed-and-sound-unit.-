⚡ Flash your ESP32

Build the firmware and flash it straight from this browser — no drivers, no Arduino IDE upload step.

  1. Disconnect the battery from the controller (GPIO12 must be free or the upload fails).
  2. Plug the ESP32 into this computer with a USB data cable.
  3. Press Build & Flash, then pick your board's serial port in the popup.
Ready. Connect your board and press Build & Flash.
